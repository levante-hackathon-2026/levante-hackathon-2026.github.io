### Information for Participants

#### Communication
Slack will be the primary method of communication during the workshop. If you have not joined our Slack Workspace, please do so! If you need another invitation, please contact us (levantehackathon@gmail.com).

In the Slack #general channel, there is a pinned "Resource Hub" that serves as a centralized spot for helpful links. 

#### Code of Conduct
All organizers, speakers, and participants are held to the event code of conduct, which can be reviewed [here](https://levante-hackathon-2026.github.io/code-of-conduct).

#### Hackathon project participants
If you applied for and were accepted to take part in the second week of the hackathon and need access to the project participant schedule or Slack channel, please contact us (levantehackathon@gmail.com).
